# EntrLabs Research Library

A public research library documenting foundations, empirical findings, critiques, and open questions associated with EntrLabs frameworks, learning models, and practices.

## EntrSL Research Collection

The first collection focuses on entrepreneurship-oriented service-learning. It combines direct EntrSL studies with transferable, foundational, and critical scholarship on reflection, reciprocity, community partnership, institutional design, and outcomes. Inclusion does not imply direct validation of EntrSL.

**Audit boundary:** 9,924 Zotero journal articles · 7,620 with abstracts · 345 direct candidates · 25 selected · 23 full texts reviewed · 2 abstract/metadata-only.

The collection is published by EntrLabs and curated by Joseph E. Iesue. The complete Zotero screening ledger, snapshots, cached abstracts, and full texts remain private.

## Repository map

- `index.md`: EntrSL collection landing page and curated shelf.
- `methodology.md`: scope, selection process, appraisal rubric, journal context, and limitations.
- `screening-log.md`: aggregate stage counts and missing-full-text decisions.
- `_papers/`: 25 individual curator analyses.
- `_data/journal_metrics.json`: dated OpenAlex source metrics and collection-specific threshold.
- `references.bib` and `CITATION.cff`: citation assets.
- `.github/workflows/pages.yml`: GitHub Pages deployment.

## Scholarly boundary

This is a curated research collection, not a systematic review, PRISMA-compliant synthesis, formal risk-of-bias assessment, or claim that the selected literature validates an EntrLabs framework. Journal metrics are descriptive context and never determine article appraisal.
