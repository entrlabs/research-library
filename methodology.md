# Methodology

## Scope and census

On 23 August 2026, the audit paginated every top-level Zotero item of type `journalArticle` without a collection, saved-search, tag, or keyword restriction. The census contained **9,924 journal articles**, including **7,620 with abstracts**. Automated title-and-abstract screening identified **345 direct candidates**, **279 near-scope records**, and **9,300 out-of-scope records** across **211 candidate journals**. The complete screening ledger is retained privately to protect Zotero-derived research data.

Screening terms covered service-learning, academic service-learning, community-engaged and community-based learning, experiential learning, work-integrated learning, learning-by-doing, entrepreneurship education, reflection, community partners, civic engagement, critical service-learning, and evidence syntheses. Automated screening created the candidate pool; it did not substitute for scholarly selection.

## Selection and evidence appraisal

The final 25 prioritize direct relevance, methodological strength, conceptual importance, stakeholder coverage, and usefulness for NEXUS/EntrSL design. Each paper receives two article-level scores:

- **EntrSL relevance (1–5):** directness to service-learning and experiential-learning design, entrepreneurship, reflection, reciprocity, outcomes, or institutional practice.
- **Evidence quality (1–5):** fit and transparency of study design, sample or synthesis breadth, analytical quality, and inferential limits.

These scores are added for ordering only. They are not a meta-analytic effect, certainty grade, or claim that unlike methods are interchangeable. Full text was reviewed for **23 papers**. Two records remain abstract/metadata-only and are explicitly flagged.

## Journal standing

Journal standing is reported separately from paper evidence quality. Metrics were retrieved from the official OpenAlex Sources API on 23 August 2026:

- **Two-year mean citedness:** OpenAlex's source-level mean citation measure for the recent two-year window.
- **h-index:** OpenAlex's source-level h-index.
- **Top journal:** at or above the 75th percentile of two-year mean citedness among the 21 journals represented by this 25-paper shortlist (threshold 7.007619).

This custom badge is **not an SJR quartile, not a Journal Citation Reports quartile, and not field-normalized**. SCImago's official page was inaccessible to the required browser during the audit, so no SJR claim is made. A high journal metric never raises an individual paper's evidence-quality score.

## Reproducibility and boundaries

The immutable working inputs were read from Zotero's local API and cached outside the repository. Zotero was not modified. Public repository artifacts include the curated analyses, aggregate audit counts, journal metric snapshot, bibliography, and methodology. The complete ledger and Zotero snapshots remain private. Selection still requires scholarly judgment, especially where adjacent experiential-learning traditions or context-specific studies compete for limited places.
