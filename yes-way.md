<section class="page-hero">
  <p class="eyebrow">The parent philosophy</p>
  <h1>Know what deserves your yes.<br><em>Practice it as a way.</em></h1>
  <p>The Yes-Way is a practical philosophy of capability, contribution, environment, and agency. Its central loop is reciprocal: disciplined capacity enables service, while attentive service develops judgment, skill, character, and awareness.</p>
</section>

<section class="four-yeses">
  <article><span>01</span><p class="eyebrow">Yes to the Mirror</p><h2>Master the instrument.</h2><p>Private discipline, self-awareness, and command of attention make public trust possible.</p></article>
  <article class="is-active"><span>02</span><p class="eyebrow">Yes to the Weight</p><h2>Become useful.</h2><p>Strength prepares service. Purposeful, bounded service develops strength in return.</p></article>
  <article><span>03</span><p class="eyebrow">Yes to the Ground</p><h2>Improve the conditions.</h2><p>People and environments interact. Diagnose the systems that shape what effort can become.</p></article>
  <article><span>04</span><p class="eyebrow">Yes to the Path</p><h2>Walk your route.</h2><p>Development cannot be delegated. Choose the next rung and climb at an honest pace.</p></article>
</section>
