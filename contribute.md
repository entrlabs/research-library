# Contribute evidence

The Yes-Way Evidence Library accepts proposed additions, corrections, replications, and stronger full-text sources for its EntrSL collection.

## What belongs

A contribution should strengthen at least one of these evidence domains: direct entrepreneurship service-learning, experiential entrepreneurship education, transferable service-learning design, reflection and assessment, reciprocal community partnership, institutional implementation, or learner and partner outcomes.

## What to provide

- Complete citation and DOI or stable URL
- Which evidence domain the paper strengthens
- Whether full text is legally available
- Study design, sample, context, principal finding, and limitations
- Any conflict of interest or relationship to the authors
- A proposed classification: direct EntrSL, experiential entrepreneurship, transferable service-learning, or foundational/institutional evidence

## Review standard

Proposals are screened using the published relevance and evidence-quality rubric. Journal standing never substitutes for paper appraisal. Inclusion remains a scholarly decision, and suggested records must not be added to or modified in Zotero without separate authorization.

For repository mechanics, see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
