# About the library

The **EntrLabs Research Library** documents the research foundations, empirical findings, critiques, and unresolved questions associated with EntrLabs frameworks, learning models, and practices.

The **EntrSL Research Collection** is its first collection. It combines direct entrepreneurship service-learning research with transferable scholarship on service-learning, experiential learning, community partnership, reflection, institutional design, and outcomes. Transferable evidence is labeled and should not be interpreted as direct validation of EntrSL.

The collection is published by EntrLabs and curated by **Joseph E. Iesue**. Twenty-three selected papers were reviewed from available full text; two are explicitly marked abstract/metadata-only. The full review boundaries are documented in the [methodology]({{ '/methodology/' | relative_url }}).
